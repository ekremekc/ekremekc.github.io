I was presenting at [SIAM2026 Annual Meeting](https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/an26/) in Cleveland, United States. 

The presentation was about our topology optimization work for prosthetic foot. All the abstracts can be accessed from [searchable abstracts file.](https://www.siam.org/media/zxqcoyej/an26_abstracts.pdf) Refer page 30 for our abstract with the title: "Topology Optimization for Iso-Complaint 3D Printable Prosthetic Foot".
